# Elena's own texts are required in French, English and Italian; works stay in their original language

Every Visitor-facing page exists in all three Languages, with the Visitor sent to the one matching their browser. Elena's own prose — Text blocks and each Entry's Description — must exist in all three before it is published; there is no fallback to French. Work titles, Poem text and Imported Entries stay in their Original language, shown with a badge, because translating a published work's title or a poem would misrepresent it, and blocking imports on translation would stall them. A "Traduire" helper that drafts the other Languages for Elena to review is planned as a later feature.

## Considered Options

- Fallback to French when a translation is missing — declined: Elena wants the three Language versions complete.
- Translate everything including titles and Poems — rejected as misrepresenting the works.
